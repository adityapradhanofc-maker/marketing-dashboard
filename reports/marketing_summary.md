## Key insights
**Overview:** ₹40.13M spend produced ₹538.91M revenue (ROI 1,243%, ROAS 13.4x) across 6 channel(s) and 24 campaign(s).

### 1. Best-performing campaigns
**Highest ROI:** Loyalty Rewards (Email) 11,368%; Cart Abandonment (Email) 8,738%; Monthly Newsletter (Email) 7,273%

**Highest revenue:** Loyalty Rewards (Email) ₹131.3M; Cart Abandonment (Email) ₹96.6M; Brand Search (Google Ads) ₹80.0M

**Weakest ROI:** Shopping Ads Q4 (Google Ads) 226%; Unboxing Series (YouTube) 243%; Lookalike Audience (Facebook) 260%

### 2. High-conversion channels
- **Email** converts best: 10.41% of clicks, at a CPA of ₹29.
- **YouTube** converts least: 2.24% (CPA ₹405).
- Most expensive customers: **LinkedIn** (CPA ₹829).

### 3. Customer engagement trends
- Highest engagement rate: **Instagram** (7.86% of impressions).
- Engagement rate rose from 4.73% (first 3 months) to 5.66% (last 3 months).
- Peak month: **November 2025** (5.99%).

### 4. Recommendations
1. **Shift budget towards Email.** It gets 8% of spend but earns 54% of revenue.
2. **Review spend on Google Ads.** It takes 47% of spend but returns only 29% of revenue; cut low-ROI campaigns before cutting the channel.
3. **Fix or pause Shopping Ads Q4 (Google Ads).** It has the lowest ROI (226%); test new creatives or targeting first.
4. **Turn Instagram engagement into sales.** It leads on engagement (7.9%) but converts at only 3.0%; add clearer calls-to-action and shoppable links.
5. **Cut acquisition cost on LinkedIn** (CPA ₹829) with tighter targeting and better landing pages.
